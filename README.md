## About the Project

Space exploration is advancing further than ever before — but the health and safety of astronauts must advance with it.

**StellarShield Health** is an intelligent health monitoring and decision-support system designed to help astronauts monitor their physical and mental well-being during long-duration space missions.

Developed for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**, the project explores how artificial intelligence, machine learning, and offline-first software architecture can support health monitoring when communication with Earth is delayed, limited, or temporarily unavailable.

Our vision is to bring a more autonomous approach to astronaut healthcare: transforming health data into meaningful insights that help crews recognize potential concerns and make more informed decisions.

> **Our mission:** Make intelligent health support accessible wherever exploration takes humanity.

---

## The Challenge

Beyond Earth's atmosphere, astronauts face an environment that places extraordinary demands on the human body and mind.

Long-duration missions introduce several challenges:

| Challenge | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Communication Delays | Immediate medical guidance from Earth may not always be available. |
| Limited Medical Resources | Crews must manage health concerns with limited onboard equipment and support. |
| Continuous Monitoring | Changes in physiological indicators need to be recognized early. |
| Isolated Environments | Health systems must remain useful even when connectivity is interrupted. |
| Mental Well-being | Sleep, stress, workload, and social isolation can affect crew performance and well-being. |

Traditional approaches that rely heavily on Earth-based assistance may not be sufficient for future deep-space exploration.

**The opportunity:** Build intelligent systems that help astronauts monitor their health and access meaningful information independently.

---

## Our Solution

StellarShield Health aims to provide an **offline-first, AI-assisted health monitoring platform** that processes health information locally and presents it through an intuitive dashboard.

The system is designed to:

- Monitor and visualize important physiological indicators.
- Analyze health measurements and identify unusual patterns.
- Apply intelligent data analysis to support anomaly detection.
- Generate understandable alerts and health insights.
- Maintain locally stored health records when connectivity is unavailable.
- Support data synchronization when communication is restored.

Rather than replacing medical professionals, StellarShield Health is intended to act as a decision-support tool, helping astronauts recognize potential health concerns and understand their health information.

## Health Parameters

The proposed monitoring framework considers multiple dimensions of astronaut health.

**Physical health**
- Heart Rate
- Blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO₂)
- Blood Pressure
- Body Temperature

**Sleep and recovery**
- Sleep Duration
- Sleep Quality
- Sleep Patterns

**Mental well-being**
- Mood Indicators
- Stress Levels
- Workload
- Social Well-being
- Fatigue Risk Indicators

**Space environment**
- Radiation Exposure

*The availability and implementation of individual measurements depend on the selected datasets, sensors, and integrations.*

## System Architecture

StellarShield Health follows an offline-first architecture centered on local data processing, health monitoring, and intelligent decision support.

### How It Works

```text
       HEALTH DATA SOURCES
                │
                ▼
       DATA COLLECTION
                │
                ▼
       LOCAL DATA STORAGE
                │
                ▼
       DATA PROCESSING
                │
                ▼
       AI & ANALYSIS ENGINE
                │
                ▼
       ANOMALY DETECTION
                │
                ▼
       HEALTH DASHBOARD
                │
                ▼
       ALERTS & HEALTH INSIGHTS
                │
                ▼
       DATA SYNCHRONIZATION
       (When connectivity returns)
```

The objective is to preserve essential monitoring and access to stored health information even when communication with Earth is unavailable.
