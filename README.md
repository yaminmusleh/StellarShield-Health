🚀 StellarShield Health

Intelligent Astronaut Health Monitoring for Long-Duration Space Missions

StellarShield Health is an AI-powered health monitoring and decision-support system designed to support astronaut well-being during long-duration space missions.

Developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, the project explores how intelligent software can help monitor astronaut health, identify unusual physiological patterns, and provide meaningful health insights — especially when communication with Earth is delayed or unavailable.

Our mission is to bring a more autonomous approach to astronaut healthcare, helping crews make informed decisions even in the most isolated environments.

⸻

🌌 The Challenge

Space missions expose astronauts to demanding conditions that can affect their physical and mental well-being.

During long-duration missions, several challenges make health monitoring particularly difficult:

* Communication delays: Immediate medical assistance from Earth cannot always be guaranteed.
* Limited medical resources: Astronauts may need to manage health concerns with limited onboard support.
* Continuous monitoring: Changes in vital signs and health patterns need to be identified early.
* Data availability: Health monitoring systems must remain functional even when communication is interrupted.

Traditional approaches that depend heavily on Earth-based support are not always sufficient for future deep-space exploration.

🛡️ Our Solution

StellarShield Health aims to provide an intelligent, offline-first health monitoring platform that processes astronaut health information locally.

The system is designed to:

* Monitor and visualize important physiological indicators.
* Analyze health measurements and identify unusual patterns.
* Detect potential anomalies using intelligent data analysis.
* Generate understandable alerts and health insights.
* Maintain local health records when connectivity is unavailable.
* Synchronize stored information when communication becomes available again.

Rather than replacing medical professionals, StellarShield Health aims to provide decision support and help astronauts recognize potential health concerns.

🧬 Health Parameters

The proposed monitoring system considers several health indicators:

* Heart Rate
* Blood Oxygen Saturation (SpO₂)
* Blood Pressure
* Body Temperature
* Sleep Patterns
* Stress Indicators
* Radiation Exposure

The availability and implementation of individual measurements will depend on the selected data sources and sensor integration.

⚙️ System Architecture

StellarShield Health follows an offline-first architecture built around local processing and data availability.

Proposed workflow:

1. Health measurements are collected or received from available data sources.
2. Measurements are stored locally.
3. The analysis engine processes the collected information.
4. Potential anomalies are identified.
5. The system generates alerts and presents health insights.
6. Data synchronization takes place when connectivity becomes available.
